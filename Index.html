🏖️ About Canyon Cove

Located along Far East Road in Piloto Wawa, Canyon Cove Hotel & Spa is one of the top beach and hotel destinations in Nasugbu, Batangas. It offers a wide white-sand shoreline, calm waters, and breathtaking sunsets over the West Philippine Sea.

🌊 White Sand Beach

Private beachfront with fine sand and scenic views of Nasugbu Bay.

🏊 Massive Pool

One of the largest recreational pools in Batangas with a kiddie area.

🛏️ Hotel Suites

Spacious Superior Twin, King, and Deluxe rooms with private balconies.

🚤 Water Sports

Jet skiing, kayaking, banana boat, and inflatable waterpark.
